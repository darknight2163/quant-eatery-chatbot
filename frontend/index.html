Our story

Quant Eatery's kitchen has always been home.

What started as a small eatery in the lanes of Mathikere has grown into a beloved neighbourhood institution. Every recipe was handed down through three generations — adapted slightly, but never compromised.

We believe that good food doesn't need a fancy setting. It just needs care, quality ingredients, and the patience to do things properly. That's still how we cook today.

Now, with Quicky — our ordering chatbot — placing a fresh meal is as easy as saying what you want.
